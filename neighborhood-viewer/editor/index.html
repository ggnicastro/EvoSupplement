Methods, limits and provenance

These exploratory associations identify candidate alternatives, not a direction of evolutionary replacement or proof of functional equivalence. A missing annotation is not evidence of biological absence. Related strains and overlapping samples can inflate support; inspect metadata and independent lineages.

Network links use Jaccard intersection / union, or weighted Jaccard from domain occurrence counts. Grouping uses the retained sparse graph. The circular drawing shows group membership; distance between nodes has no biological meaning. Order patterns preserve gene boundaries, strand relative to the query, and unknown genes. Compact inputs provide order, not measured genomic distance.

Alternative-domain tests use one-sided Fisher depletion and Benjamini–Hochberg adjustment across the eligible tested pairs. Context evidence compares query-relative positions and neighboring gene architectures separately from statistical association.
