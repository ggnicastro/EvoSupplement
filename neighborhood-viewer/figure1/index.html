Filter syntax

Include: every term must match. Exclude: a match to any term removes that neighborhood.

Plain text searches all indexed fields. Field filters and quoted phrases work in both boxes.

domain:RDD organism:Streptomyces pfam:FHA taxon:"Actinomycetota" pid:WP_

Fields: domain, pfam, organism, taxon, pid, nucleotide, product, assembly, and block.
